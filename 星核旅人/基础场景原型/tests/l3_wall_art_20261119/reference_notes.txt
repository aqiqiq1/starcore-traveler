What I actually observed in sotn_outerwall_stone_crop.png:
  - The wall is divided by tall, light vertical pilasters; the bays between them
    are darker brick and carry the arched openings/ledges.
  - The pilasters read as one solid light member with a lit edge and a dark
    return; the bays change tone from one bay to the next.
  - The plinth/ledge tops are the lightest lines, the undersides are dark; the
    base has a light top course, a darker body and a dark contact where it
    meets the ground.
  - The wall is built from large blocks; small chips/cracks are 1 px and sparse,
    not a noise field.
  - The entrance frame (sotn_entrance_60s.jpg) is only 320x240 and dark; it
    supports the "dark opening in a thick wall" reading, not pixel detail.

Borrowed (structure only):
  - vertical bay rhythm; light pilaster against darker bays;
  - top-lit / bottom-dark base and cornice reading;
  - a few 1 px chips and a darker contact band.

Not borrowed:
  - SotN's gothic black/red palette, moon, window tracery, specific patterns or
    any pixels; the L3 wall stays the project's warm grey stone.

Applied boundary:
  - The visible right wall (world 2320..2612, camera shows to 2520) gets the
    light pilaster, three bay tones, 1 px chips and the base contact. Return
    side and corner column keep their exact old drawing.
  - The 320x240 entrance frame is not used to claim camera motion or animation;
    it is a static supporting frame only.
  - I did not re-download the source video; the local crops are the same ones
    the coordinator recorded, at their saved resolution.

Unverified:
  - Continuous camera/occlusion behaviour of the SotN source (static crops
    only). The L3 occlusion values here are measured from this project's own
    gate code, not inferred from SotN.
