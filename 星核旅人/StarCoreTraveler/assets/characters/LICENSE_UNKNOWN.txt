The provided material does not include a character license or an author
statement. The copy is for this internal course prototype only. Do not publish
or redistribute these character assets until the project owner supplies
documented permission covering the intended release.

The original sheets are 128x128 per frame with transparent padding. The
prototype does not treat the 128 px canvas as the character's visible size:
the visible body is roughly 33-38 px high and the collision body is 12x30.

The walk sheet is used for the default 10-frame walk state. Run is triggered
only by left Shift or a same-direction double tap, not by a separate character
file. No character-design sheet, combo sheet, zip, or author preview was copied
into the project.
